# kurtz name, logos and product identity

Ralf Hauser maintains the kurtz product identity. Open-source copyright licenses
do not by themselves grant permission to imply an official kurtz release,
endorsement or affiliation. This policy claims no registered trademark status
and is subject to applicable law, including descriptive and other lawful uses.

## Permitted references

You may truthfully discuss kurtz, link to the project, say your software is based
on or compatible with it, and show screenshots with the applicable media credits.
Personal builds and unchanged official redistribution may be identified
accurately. Do not imply that Ralf provides support for your distribution.

For a modified or independently branded public product, use a distinct name,
logo and application icon. A factual “based on kurtz” credit is welcome. Do not
use the kurtz or ralleur identity in a way that suggests your release, domain,
store listing or service is official without permission.

## Scope and provenance

The identity includes the kurtz name, curled-z wordmark, symbol and official app
icons documented in [marketing/brand](marketing/brand/README.md), including
their copies in Apple asset catalogs and `website/assets`. The ralleur identity
under `marketing/brand/ralleur` belongs to the broader Ralleur project identity;
its transfer must be expressly agreed and is not implied by a sale of kurtz.

This is a policy about product identity, not a retroactive withdrawal of
copyright permissions. Previously licensed code or artwork retains its grants.
Brand-generation code keeps its source license. Fonts, film frames, screenshots
and other third-party material keep their own licenses and credits; see
[media sources](marketing/sources-and-licenses.md) and
[font notices](Shared/Resources/KurtzThirdPartyNotices.txt).
AI-generated backgrounds are identified in the existing provenance records;
this policy does not assert exclusive copyright in them.

Swiftfin, Jellyfin, Apple and other names and marks belong to their respective
owners. The project's acknowledgment of them grants no rights in their brands.
For uses beyond these permissions, contact Ralf through the kurtz repository.
