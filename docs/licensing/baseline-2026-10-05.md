# Initial rights inventory — 5 October 2026

Reviewer: Codex, repository evidence review at Ralf's request. This is a
record of the observed integration, not Ralf's signature, an independent legal
opinion, or a conclusion that every historical contribution belongs to him.

## Foundation and package graph

- `LICENSE.md` is the unchanged MPL-2.0 text inherited from Swiftfin. Git history
  contains many upstream authors. No historical CLA acceptance is presumed.
- Application SwiftPM versions and transitive pins are recorded in
  `Swiftfin.xcodeproj/project.xcworkspace/xcshareddata/swiftpm/Package.resolved`.
  `docs/release/source-index.json` records matching immutable source archives
  for all of those pins and native engine/build recipes.
- `Shared/Resources/KurtzThirdPartyNotices.txt` retains package license texts,
  including MIT, BSD, Apache, MPL and LGPL notices. These are component terms,
  not evidence of an assignment to Ralf. The test and macro package lockfiles
  are separate inputs and may use other revisions; do not treat them as the
  shipping application's lockfile.
- `docs/release/README.md` records the GPL-3.0-or-later combined distribution,
  MPL source notices, libVLC and GPL-enabled mpv, FFmpeg, native recipes and
  source-delivery obligations. The MPVUI artifact/input locks remain authoritative
  for exact binary downloads, rather than wrapper package license labels.

## Assets and build tools

- Sora and Noto font terms are retained in `Shared/Resources/Fonts`; web font
  copies keep their OFL notices. Film-derived artwork and compositions have
  CC BY / CC BY-SA obligations in `marketing/sources-and-licenses.md`.
- Brand provenance is recorded in `marketing/brand/README.md`; generated DMG
  backgrounds must not be represented as exclusively human-authored artwork.
- Homebrew, Ruby/Fastlane, Python DMG helpers, Node rendering tools, workflows
  and Go proof-of-concept modules are recorded as build/development inputs.
  Installing a tool does not transfer its rights. A future decision to bundle
  one requires a fresh review of that actual redistribution and its transitives.
- Any in-progress Mutti Connect pin is an additional native-code input, not a
  consequence of the kurtz CLA. Its Go modules, embedded third-party code,
  exact source archive and notices must be included in a release review.

   checked-out source and is absent from the bundled license-text sections.
   No license/copyright grant was found in the inspected source headers.
   Its public availability and use by Swiftfin do not establish redistribution
   permission. Resolve with an applicable upstream license/grant covering that
   revision, a correctly licensed replacement, or removal. Do not invent MIT

2. If the working tree includes the new Mutti Connect native bridge, the existing

These findings do not modify or withdraw past releases. The dependency register
