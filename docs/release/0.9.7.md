# kurtz 0.9.7 Beta

This release completes the remaining in-app branding: current-product references
in 32 translation files now say kurtz, including settings and error messages.
Sora is used for app-owned text styles while retaining platform text sizes and
Dynamic Type. System-owned menus and monospaced diagnostic text keep their
native styling.

The iPhone, iPad and Apple TV players now share the ivory curl and yellow rays.
The small upper-left mark appears with hidden controls and yields to playback
controls and supplements. Apple TV also has a native launch storyboard with the
kurtz wordmark. App icons, Top Shelf and account/settings screens retain the
same identity introduced in 0.9.6.

- Version 0.9.7; Mac/iOS build 9, tvOS build 75.
- Mac requires macOS 15.6+, iPhone/iPad iOS/iPadOS 18.6+, Apple TV tvOS 26.1+.
- Existing bundle IDs, accounts, preferences and the `vela://` compatibility
  scheme are retained. Historical releases and license attributions are unchanged.
- The Apple TV installer now reads the built app's identity, selects its hardware
  UDID and verifies signatures and branding before updating the paired device.
- CI checks translated current-product names and the contents of packaged apps
  to catch the gaps missed by compilation alone.

The Mac download and matching sources are on the
[release page](https://github.com/ralleur/kurtz/releases/tag/kurtz-0.9.7).
Mobile/TV source builds and CI archives do not imply a Store or TestFlight release.
Exact build, installation and publication results are recorded separately in the
release verification record.
